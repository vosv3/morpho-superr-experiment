The problem

A serious diagnosis is overwhelming.

A letter with your test results, a miracle cure IG reel, a friend of a friend’s advice, a result in an app, a random call at 2am. What if something could remember all of it?

The moment

Every letter, every result. Ask it anything.

Claude can hold more in view than any one person can keep in their head. Give it your records, and every letter, result and scan stays in view at once.

It explains. It never diagnoses, never advises, never replaces the person treating you.

Morpho is a set of instructions and templates for organising your own records and preparing your own questions. What Morpho does not do, and what it is

Morpho supplements your medical care. It never replaces it. It organises, prepares and explains. It does not diagnose, does not recommend or discourage any treatment, and is not a substitute for advice from your medical team. If something is wrong right now, call your emergency number. Questions, stuck, or built something? Write to james+morpho@vagabondingmedia.com. A person replies.
